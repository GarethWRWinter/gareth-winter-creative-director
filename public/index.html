About

Sport built the discipline.
Creativity built the brands.

In 2012, Sir Bradley Wiggins crossed the line on the Champs-Élysées as the first British winner of the Tour de France, then won Olympic gold weeks later in London. I was at Sky, part of the team that told that story to the nation. Within ten years, 1.7 million people in the UK had got on a bike for the first time. That's what storytelling does when it earns its place in the world — and I've been chasing that feeling ever since.

Team Sky ran on one idea: marginal gains. Improve every element by a single percent and the compound effect is absurd. James Clear later built half of Atomic Habits around it. For me it wasn't a concept in a book — it was Tuesday. It became the operating system behind everything I've built: at every scale, inside genuinely high-performance organisations, the discipline doesn't change and the results follow.

I'm not just a director of people. I shoot, write, direct, edit, design and produce — and I lead teams who are better than me at all of it, which is rather the point. At Mindvalley I'm making work for an audience genuinely trying to change their lives. Three million of them, across one hundred and ninety countries. People who flew to Amsterdam from every corner of the planet to be in a room together. That audience has no patience for anything dishonest, and they'll share everything that's real. Authenticity isn't a value I put on a wall. It's a method. It's also just how I'd rather work.

Artificial intelligence

who still does the work.

Most AI conversations are either breathless or terrified. Mine is neither. I treat AI as creative infrastructure — a way to take the friction out of production so the people stay free to do the part that actually matters: taste, judgement, and the decision about what's good enough to ship.

Systems, not gimmicks

I architect repeatable creative pipelines — research, generation, review, ship — so a small team produces like a big one without the quality falling off a cliff.

Human-first by design
