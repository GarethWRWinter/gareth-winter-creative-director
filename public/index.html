01 · The short version

There’s a tired old idea that work can be beautiful or it can sell. I’ve spent twenty years cheerfully proving it can do both.

I run the creative at Mindvalley, one of the world’s best-known personal growth platforms. I work directly with the CEO and steer more than a million dollars of ad spend a month, so reading a P&L is part of the day job.

I still write, shoot, direct and design. I build the AI systems that let a small team produce like a big one. And I hire people who are better than me, then do my best to stay out of their way.

Every brand I’ve worked on ended up with work worth sharing and numbers worth showing. That, in the end, is the whole job.

03 · Method

Marginal gains, applied to creative.

Team Sky ran on one idea: improve every element by one percent and the compound effect is faintly ridiculous. I was at Sky when it took a Briton to the top of the Tour de France for the first time. I’ve applied the same idea to every brand since, across four jobs that happen to make each other better.

  1. 01

    Creative direction

    Set the standard, then hold the line.

    Identity, art direction, film, design. I’ve never asked a team to clear a bar I couldn’t clear myself, and twenty years in I’m still in the edit at midnight. Standards are a habit you keep when nobody’s watching.

  2. 02

    Content & growth

    A Head of Content who can read a P&L.

    Lovely work that doesn’t move a number is a hobby, and I’ve got enough hobbies. I wire content to the commercials (positioning, channels, the full funnel) and show you the point on the graph where it started paying for itself.

  3. 03

    AI systems

    Build the machine, keep the soul.

    I design AI-powered creative systems, and the unglamorous plumbing under them, so teams ship more without shipping worse. Used badly, AI carpet-bombs the internet with beige. I have strong opinions about avoiding that.

  4. 04

    Leadership & culture

    People do brave work when they feel safe.

    I build teams from scratch, with clarity instead of politics, honesty delivered kindly, and enough rope for good people to surprise you. I care about the work because I care about the people making it.

Most AI conversations are either breathless or terrified. I’m too busy using the stuff to be either. I treat it as creative infrastructure: it takes the donkey-work out of production so people stay free for taste, judgement and knowing when something is good enough to ship.

07 · Where it started

Sport built the discipline. Creativity built the brands.

In 2012 Sir Bradley Wiggins became the first Briton to win the Tour de France, then won Olympic gold in London a few weeks later for good measure. I was at Sky, part of the team that told that story to the nation.

Within ten years, 1.7 million people in the UK had got on a bike for the first time. That’s what storytelling can do when it earns its place, and I’ve been chasing the feeling ever since.

Marginal gains was never a concept in a book for me. It was just Tuesday. It’s still the operating system: earn trust before asking for the sale, do fewer things properly, and keep it honest, because the audiences I make work for can smell a phoney a mile off and will share anything that’s real.
